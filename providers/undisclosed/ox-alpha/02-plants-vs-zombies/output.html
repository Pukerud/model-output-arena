MINI PLANTS vs ZOMBIES

Defend your lawn! Collect sun, plant defenses and survive every wave of the undead.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Collect sun — click falling sun and sunflower sparks to earn energy.
  2. Plant defenders — drag or click a seed packet, then click a lawn tile.
  3. Stop the zombies — Peashooters shoot, Wall-nuts block, Snow Peas chill, Cherry Bombs explode.
  4. Lawnmowers save each lane once — after that, one breach ends the run.
  5. Survive all waves to win big bonus points and enter the leaderboard.
