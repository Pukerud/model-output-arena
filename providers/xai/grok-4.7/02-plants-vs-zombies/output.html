Lawn Last Light

A compact lane defense. Collect sun, plant a row, and do not let the horde reach the house.

How to play

  • Click falling sun, or sun from sunflowers, before you spend it.
  • Select a seed packet, then click an empty lawn tile. Keys 1–5 select packets.
  • Peashooters fire down the lane. Snow peas slow. Wall-nuts stall. Cherries burst.
  • Each lane has one mower. After that, a zombie at the house ends the night.
  • Esc pauses. Your best scores stay in this browser.
