Little Lane of the Dead

a small plant vs zombie game · v1.0

How to Play

Goal. Stop the zombies from reaching your house across three lawns.

• Click a seed packet, then click a lawn tile to plant. Right-click cancels.
• ☼ Sun falls from the sky and is produced by Sunflowers — click it to collect.
• 🌻 Sunflower (50) makes sun. 🍃 Peashooter (100) shoots. 🧴 Wall-nut (50) just endures.
• 🖱 Shovel digs a plant back up (no refund). Each row has one lawn mower — a free, one-time save.
• The red flag marks a HUGE WAVE. Survive all three lawns to win.
