How to play

☀ Click suns to collect them.
🌻 Sunflowers make sun. Peashooters shoot. Wall-nuts stall. Cherry Bombs clear a patch.
Click a seed packet, then click a lawn tile to plant. Packets recharge after use.
🧟 Zombies come in waves. Each lane has one lawnmower — it only fires once.
Survive all waves to win. Your score goes on the leaderboard. P = pause.
