Lawn Defense

A small plant-versus-zombie game with menus, a persistent leaderboard, five lanes, sunny seed packets, and a tiny strategic plan.

Click seed packets to choose a plant, then click an empty lawn tile. Collect falling sun to fund your defense.

How to Play

The plan: bank sun, plant Sunflowers, place Peashooters in every lane, use Wall-nuts as shields, and clear crowded lanes with Cherry Bombs.

  • Choose a seed packet from the bottom bar.
  • Click an empty lawn tile to plant it.
  • Click falling sun tokens to collect 25 sun each.
  • Peashooters fire at zombies in their own lane.
  • Wall-nuts soak damage while your shooters keep firing.
  • Cherry Bombs explode after a short fuse and damage nearby zombies.
  • Survive five waves without a zombie reaching the house.
