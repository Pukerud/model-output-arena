How to play

Sun falls from the sky and sunflowers make more of it. Plant defenders on the left of the lawn; zombies walk in from the right. If a zombie gets past a row's lawn mower, the game is over. One shovel, four plants, twelve waves.

The build plan

  1. Core loop — one canvas, one requestAnimationFrame loop, fixed timestep.
  2. Grid + planting — 5×9 cells, hit test to cell, cost + recharge per seed.
  3. Sun economy — sky drops, sunflowers generate, click to bank.
  4. Plant roster — sunflower, peashooter, repeater, snow pea, wall-nut, cherry bomb.
  5. Zombies — shared brain, per-type stats, armour that breaks off.
  6. Waves — flag waves, big waves, a lull timer between pushes.
  7. Lawn mowers — one per row, single use, the fail state.
  8. Meta — title, loadout, difficulty, pause, results, leaderboard.
  9. Juice — squash, screen shake, particles, synthesised sound.
