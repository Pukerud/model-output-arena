GARDEN
SIEGEPLANTS VS ZOMBIES

Endless survival • Hold the lawn

Sun is your currency. Plant your defenses, harvest sunlight, and stop every shambling wave before it reaches the house.

Keyboard: 1-5 plants • Space pause

How to Play

  • Collect sun. Click falling suns or the ones your sunflowers make. Everything is paid for in sun.
  • Plant seeds. Pick a card, then click an empty lawn tile. Cards recharge between uses.
  • Hold the lanes. Peashooters attack forward. Wall-nuts stall. Snow Peas slow. Cherry Bombs clear a crowd.
  • Last resort. Each lane has one lawnmower. It fires once, then that lane is unprotected.
  • Survive. Waves keep coming and get meaner. Score points for kills and wave milestones.
  • Leaderboard. Your top eight runs are saved locally in this browser.
