How to Play

Controls — click a seed packet then click a lawn square to plant. Right-click (or the shovel) removes a plant. Esc pauses. Click the falling suns to collect them; sunflowers make sun on their own. Zombies walk in from the street on the right; a huge wave is announced before it arrives. Every plant stops a zombie in its row — but a zombie that reaches the house ends the run.
