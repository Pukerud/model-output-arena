Plants vs Zombies

Arena Edition · Vanilla Canvas Game
Project Architecture Plan

1. Grid & Spatial Partitioning

5-row by 9-column grid (550px height / 1000px width). Coordinate mapping converts screen click coordinates into discrete cell indices [row, col].

2. Entity Component Simulation

Object-oriented entity lifecycle for Plants, Zombies, Projectiles, Suns, and Lawnmowers. State machines handle planting cooldowns, projectile splats, plant bite damage, and zombie armor states.

3. Sun Economy & Producer Loops

Ambient natural sunlight spawns randomly from the sky every 8s. Sunflowers pulse and yield 25 sun every 14s. Clicking suns triggers smooth bezier floating interpolation into the player's bank.

4. Combat & Armor Degradation

Lane-filtered collision checks ensure peashooters only fire when zombies occupy their row. Zombies support armor degradation (Conehead, Buckethead) and Wall-nuts show visual damage cracking.

5. Zero-Dependency Web Audio Synthesizer

Custom procedural Web Audio sound effects: pea shot pops, zombie bites, sun collect arpeggios, mower rumblings, and cherry bomb blast.
