Plants vs Zombies

Mini Lawn Defense — protect your house from the shambling horde

How to Play

Goal

  • Zombies enter from the right and shamble toward your house on the left.
  • Collect sun (click falling suns and the ones your sunflowers make) and spend it on plants.
  • Each lane has one lawn mower. If a zombie reaches it, it clears the lane — once.
  • If a zombie gets past a lane with no mower, the zombies eat your brains.
  • Adventure: survive 10 waves. Endless: see how far you can go.

Controls

  • Click a seed card (or 1–7), then click a lawn tile.
  • S or the shovel card removes a plant.
  • Right-click or Esc deselects. P pauses. M mutes.

Plants

  • Sunflower (50) — makes 25 sun every 24 s.
  • Peashooter (100) — shoots peas down its lane.
  • Snow Pea (175) — frozen peas slow zombies.
  • Repeater (200) — two peas per volley.
  • Wall-nut (50) — very tough; blocks the lane.
  • Potato Mine (25) — arms in 14 s, then one big bang.
  • Cherry Bomb (150) — explodes in a 3×3 area.

Zombies

  • Zombie — plain, slow, hungry.
  • Conehead — the cone soaks up damage.
  • Buckethead — the bucket soaks up a lot of damage.
  • Runner — twice as fast, but fragile.
  • Flag Zombie — leads every 5th (huge) wave.
