LAWN DEFENCE

Plant your garden. Hold the line. Keep the brains in your head.


12 waves  •  6 plants  •  5 kinds of zombie

How to Play

Collect sun — it falls from the sky and sunflowers make more. Click it before it fades.

Pick a seed packet, then click a lawn tile to plant. Survive all 12 waves.

Each lane has one lawn mower. It saves you once. After that, a zombie that walks off the left edge ends the run.
