PLANTS vs ZOMBIES

Mini Arena — endless wave survival. Collect sun, plant defenders, and keep the horde off your lawn.

🏆 LEADERBOARD

    How to Play

    ☀ Click falling sun (and Sunflower sun) to bank it.

    🌻 Pick a seed card, then click a lawn tile to plant. Cards recharge over time — watch the cooldown shade.

    🦰 Zombies march left and eat your plants. If one reaches your house, that row's lawnmower fires once. Lose all mowers' rows to a zombie and it's over.

    Sunflower 50 · Peashooter 100 · Snowpea 175 (slows) · Wall-nut 50 · Cherry Bomb 150 (3×3 blast)

    P/Esc pause  · M mute  · right-click deselects a card. Waves get bigger forever — how long can you last?

    ❚❚ PAUSED

    Press P or Esc to resume.
