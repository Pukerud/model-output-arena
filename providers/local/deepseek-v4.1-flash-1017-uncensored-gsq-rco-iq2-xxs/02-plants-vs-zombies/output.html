Lawn of the Dead

A small, polished Plants vs Zombies game. Defend five lanes with plants, collect sun, and survive eight waves.

Difficulty:

Plan

The build plan this game follows.

  1. Build a single-file HTML shell with menu, HUD, canvas board, and card bar.
  2. Lay out a 5 x 9 lawn grid and define sun, plants, zombies, and lawnmower state.
  3. Implement sun drops, sunflower production, and click-to-collect economy.
  4. Implement plant cards with costs, cooldowns, and per-plant behaviour.
  5. Implement zombie spawning in waves, eating, bullets, and damage.
  6. Add per-row lawnmowers as the last line of defence.
  7. Add win/loss handling, score, difficulty scaling, and a persistent leaderboard.
  8. Polish visuals, then verify the game runs and interacts correctly.

How to play

Click a plant card, then click a lawn tile to plant it.

  • Click falling sun to collect it. Sunflowers also produce sun.
  • Peashooters damage zombies in their own lane.
  • Wall-nuts absorb damage and stall zombies.
  • Cherry Bombs explode and damage nearby zombies.
  • Each lane has one lawnmower. It triggers once and clears that lane.
  • Survive all eight waves to win; if a zombie passes the left edge without a mower, you lose.
