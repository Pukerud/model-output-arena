How to Play

☀️ Click falling suns (and sun produced by Sunflowers) to earn sun.

🌱 Click a seed packet, then click a lawn tile to plant. Right-click cancels.

🌻 Sunflower makes sun · 🟢 Peashooter shoots · 🥜 Wall-nut tanks · 🍒 Cherry Bomb blows up a 3×3 area.

🚜 Each row has one lawn mower — your last line of defense.

⏸ Press P to pause. Beat all 10 waves to win!
