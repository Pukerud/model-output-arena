PLANTS vs ZOMBIES

— a tiny lawn defense —
Keyboard: 1–5 select plant · Esc pause · M mute

How to Play

  • 🌻 Sunflower (50) — grows sun. Click falling sun to bank it.
  • 🟢 Peashooter (100) — shoots peas at zombies in its lane.
  • ❄️ Snow Pea (175) — like a peashooter, but slows zombies.
  • 🥜 Wall-Nut (50) — tough. Blocks the horde.
  • 🍒 Cherry Bomb (150) — 3×3 boom. Use sparingly.
  • ⛏️ The shovel digs up a plant. Click a card, then a lawn tile.
  • Zombies march from the right. If one reaches your house, it's over.
  • Survive all 32 waves of the horde to win. Score is saved to the leaderboard!
