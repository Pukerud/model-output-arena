How to play

the whole game in six lines

☀️Click a falling sun to bank it. Sun is the only currency. 🌻Pick a seed packet, then click a lawn tile. Sunflower brews more sun. 🟢Peashooters fire down their row. Snow Peas chill zombies so they crawl. 🥜Wall-nuts have no attack but absurd patience. Buy time with them. 🍒Cherry Bombs blow up a 3×3 patch, then leave. Emergency only. ⛏Shovel to dig a plant back up. Each row has a one-shot lawnmower.
