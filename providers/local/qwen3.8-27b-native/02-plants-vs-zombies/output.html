LAWN DEFENDER

a tiny plants vs zombies · 10 waves · 5 plants · don't let them eat your brains
Keys: 1–5 select plant · S shovel · P / Esc pause
Click a card, then click the lawn. Click suns to collect them.
single-file build · no assets · no libraries

How to Play

🌻
Sunflower — 50Puts out 25 sun every few seconds. Your economy. Plant these first.
🌱
Peashooter — 100Shoots peas at any zombie in its lane. Your backbone.
❄️
Snow Pea — 175Like a peashooter, but its peas chill zombies and slow them down.
🥜
Wall-Nut — 50A tough wall that buys time. 2,000 HP of pure nut.
🍒
Cherry Bomb — 150Booms after 1 second, wrecking everything in a 3×3 area. Long reseed time.
🧟
ZombiesWalker (10 pts) · Conehead (20) · Buckethead (30, slow but huge). They march left and eat any plant they touch.
🚜
LawnmowersOne per lane. If a zombie reaches your yard's edge, the mower mows that whole lane. When it's gone, there's no backup.
☀️
SunFalls from the sky and pops out of sunflowers. Click it before it fades. 25 each.
