🌻 Mini Plants vs. Zombies 🧟

Catch falling ☀️ sun, plant defenders on your lawn, and stop the zombies from reaching your house. Survive as many waves as you can. Higher waves set a higher score.

Plants:
🌻 Sunflower — produces sun (50)
🫛 Pea Shooter — fires peas (100)
🧔 Wall-nut — soaks damage (50)
❄️ Snow Pea — slow zombies (125)
💣 Cherry Bomb — big boom (150)

Click plants to select, then click a lawn tile to plant. Click dropped sun to collect it.
