🌻 Plants vs Zombies 🧟

Mini Garden Defense Edition

How to Play

🌻 Click a plant card, then click a lawn tile to place it.

☀️ Collect falling sun and sun from Sunflowers for resources.

🔫 Peashooters fire at zombies automatically.

🧊 Snow Peas slow down zombies.

💣 Cherry Bombs destroy all zombies nearby!

🛡️ Wall-nuts block zombies with high health.

🏆 Survive 5 waves to win! Don't let zombies reach your house!
