How to Play

  • Collect sun by clicking it. It falls from the sky and is grown by Sunflowers.
  • Click a seed card, then click a lawn tile to plant. Cards recharge over time.
  • Peashooters & Snow Peas fire at zombies in their row. Wall-nuts hold the line. Cherry Bombs blow things up.
  • Survive the waves. If a zombie reaches your house, you lose — unless a lawn mower is still there.
  • Use the shovel to dig up a plant you placed wrong.
