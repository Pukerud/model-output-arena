One night. Four legends.

Every face, costume, finger joint, gravestone, tree, and cobblestone is built in code. The cast is deliberately stylized, not photoreal scanned likenesses.

An eight-phrase, 64-count Thriller-inspired routine combines zombie walks, shoulder hits, side slides, claw poses, turns, and kicks. Scrub to any count. Drag to take over the camera; scroll to dolly.

The optional pulse is original synthesized percussion, not a recording of the song. Three.js and its rendering add-ons load from a pinned CDN. All art is procedural.
