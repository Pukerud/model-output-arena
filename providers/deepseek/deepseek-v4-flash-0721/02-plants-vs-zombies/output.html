🌻 Lawn Defenders

A mini Plants vs Zombies tribute. Plant your defenses, harvest sun, and stop the horde from reaching your house.

How to Play

🖱 Click a seed packet at the top to select a plant, then click a lawn square to plant it.
☀️ Sun falls from the sky and is grown by Sunflowers — click it to collect (25).
🌱 Plants: Sunflower (50), Peashooter (100), Snow Pea (175), Wall-nut (50), Cherry Bomb (150).
🧟 Zombies march in from the right. If one reaches the house, you lose!
🚜 Lawnmowers are your last resort — one per lane, used automatically.
⚔️ Survive 3 rounds to win the level, then a harder level begins.
🧹 Shovel removes a plant. P pauses, M mutes.
