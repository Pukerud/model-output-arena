SPROUT DEFENSE

A tiny Plants-vs-Zombies in one HTML file

Shambling things are walking at your house from the right. Plant sunflowers for sun, peashooters for firepower, and wall-nuts to slow the party down. Sun is the only currency; everything else is panic.

Tip: nothing can be planted on an occupied tile — dig it up with the shovel.

HOW TO PLAY

Six plants, five lanes, one lawn

Sun falls from the sky (click the coins) and sunflowers make more. Every plant costs sun. Zombies walk left along their lane; when they reach a plant they stop and eat it. Lose a lane and the mower fires once — after that, one more bite and it's over.

Bestiary

Shambler — slow, 100 HP. Cone Head — 220 HP, wears a traffic cone. Bucket Head — 380 HP and a metal bucket that rings when you shoot it. Sprinter — 90 HP but nearly twice the pace. Giant — 1100 HP, slow, eats three plants at once. Kill a giant and the score jumps.
