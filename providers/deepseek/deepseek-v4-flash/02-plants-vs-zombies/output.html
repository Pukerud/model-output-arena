Mini Arena

🧟Zombies are coming from the right. Don't let them reach the house! 🌱Pick a seed packet above the lawn, then click a tile to plant it. ☀️Click falling suns to collect them — they are your currency. 🚜Each lane has one Lawn Mower as a last resort. It only fires once!

Survive escalating waves, level after level. Scores are saved on this device.

PLANT GUIDE

🌻 Sunflower (50) — produces bonus sun over time. The heart of your economy.

🌱 Peashooter (100) — shoots peas at every zombie in its lane.

❄️ Snow Pea (175) — peas that also chill zombies, slowing them down.

🥜 Wall-nut (50) — a tough wall that buys your shooters precious time.

🍒 Cherry Bomb (150) — explode it in a crowd. Wipes out everything nearby.

⛏️ Shovel — dig up a plant you no longer need (no refund).

Tip: sun falls from the sky all the time — don't let it expire on the lawn. Levels get harder: zombies get cone & bucket helmets and more HP.
